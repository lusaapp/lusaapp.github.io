The app

Lusa is an on-device wedding invitation studio. You choose a stationery template, import or take a photo (or keep a text-only layout), edit the couple names, date, and venue, preview the suite, and render one invitation. A finished suite can be marked ready as PNG, JPG, or PDF on the result page, and you can save a card image to Photos. Lusa has no account, no cloud library, and no social feed.

If you do not agree, do not enter the studio. You can remove the local data with Clear All Data in Settings, then delete Lusa from your device.

What stays on this device

Lusa does not create an account. The following stay in local storage on the current installation:

  • Your invitation draft: template name, couple names, date, venue, a photo label, and any JPEG you imported or captured, kept in the app’s private folder.
  • Finished suites you rendered, separate from the scripted Demo suites.
  • Your Credit balance, the welcome-gift claimed flag, and StoreKit transaction identifiers already granted.
  • Your answers to the in-app camera, photo, and microphone prompts.

Uninstalling Lusa or using Clear All Data deletes this local snapshot. Demo suites (Eleanor & Julian, Château Monogram, and Minimalist Whisper) are built into the app, marked Demo, and are not your content.

Credits and purchases

Rendering one invitation spends 1 Credit. Preview does not spend a Credit. Exporting PNG, JPG, or PDF of a suite that has already been rendered does not spend another Credit. A new render after you edit again spends 1 Credit if you confirm it.

Credits are consumable and are stored only on this device. They cannot be restored through the App Store, transferred to another device, or recovered after you clear data or remove the app. The first time you reach the home screen, after the tracking and notification dialogs, Lusa offers a New User Gift Pack with 10 Credits. That pack has only Claim. It is granted once per installation unless you clear all data.
