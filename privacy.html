Privacy Policy

Lusa · Effective October 13, 2026

Who this covers

Lusa is a wedding invitation studio that runs on your device. There is no Lusa account and no Lusa server that receives your invitations. This policy describes what the app stores locally, what it does not collect, and how Apple is involved when you buy a gift pack.

On the entry screen, Create Invitation stays unavailable until you check that you have read these Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy. You can open either document before checking the box. Opening a document does not check the box, and checking the box does not create an account.

Data stored on this device

Lusa keeps one local snapshot. It can include:

  • Onboarding completion, and whether you have already answered the tracking dialog, the notification dialog, and the New User Gift Pack.
  • The current draft: template name, couple names, ceremony date, venue, a short photo label, and, when you import or take a photo, a JPEG copy stored in the app’s private folder.
  • Finished invitations you rendered on this device.
  • Your Credit balance and the StoreKit transaction identifiers that have already been granted, so the same purchase is not applied twice.
  • Whether you allowed or denied the in-app camera, photo-library, and microphone prompts.

That snapshot is not uploaded by Lusa. Clearing all data or deleting the app removes it from the device.

Data Lusa does not collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number, or password for Lusa.
  • No guest list and no address book.
  • No analytics, advertising profile, or cross-app tracking.
  • No photos or audio files are uploaded. A photo you import or capture stays in the app’s private folder. Dictation uses the microphone and speech recognition to fill text fields, and Lusa does not save an audio recording.
  • No notifications are scheduled or delivered, even if you allow the notification dialog.

Permissions

Tracking (ATT). The first time the home screen appears, Lusa shows a tracking dialog. Allow and Don’t Allow do the same thing. Lusa does not read the advertising identifier, does not track activity across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not reorder templates or Demo suites based on the answer.

Notifications. Immediately after the tracking dialog, Lusa shows a notification dialog. Lusa does not register for push or local notifications and does not send a message when a render or a purchase finishes. Completion stays on the invitation page.

Camera. Only Capture Portrait with Camera asks. If you allow it, Lusa opens the camera and stores the picture you take as a JPEG in the app’s private folder. Don’t Allow leaves the draft editable and still offers a library import or a text-only layout. The simulator or a device without a camera explains that the camera is unavailable and does not pretend a photo was taken.

Photo library. Import from Photo Library asks to read photos, then opens the system picker so you can choose one image. That image is copied into the app’s private folder. Save Suite to Photos asks separately for permission to add a picture, then writes the finished card into your library. Refusing a save does not remove the suite already stored in the app, and the on-page PNG, JPG, and PDF note still works.

Microphone and speech recognition. Only Tap to Dictate asks. If you allow the microphone and speech recognition, Lusa listens for about four seconds and places the recognized words into the first empty couple, date, or venue field so you can edit them before review. Don’t Allow leaves those fields as manual text. Lusa does not keep an audio file.

If you deny camera, photos, or microphone, Lusa does not show that dialog again until you use the same button, and then it only explains that the choice is stored. These prompts are not shown on the first home screen.

Purchases

Gift Shop purchases are consumable Credits processed by Apple’s StoreKit. Lusa receives the verified transaction identifier and product identifier so it can add Credits once. Lusa does not receive your card number. The Credit balance and those transaction identifiers remain on this device. They are not restored after Clear All Data or after the app is removed. The New User Gift Pack adds 10 Credits locally when you tap Claim. It is not an App Store charge.

Before you purchase, the pack detail page shows how many Credits that pack includes. The shop list itself shows the pack name and price only.

Demo content

Creations includes scripted Demo suites: Eleanor & Julian, Château Monogram, and Minimalist Whisper. They are sample stationery shipped with the app, marked Demo, and they are not taken from your photos or contacts. Their order does not change if you allow or deny tracking.

Third parties

Lusa does not sell or share your draft, invitations, or Credit balance with a third party. Apple processes the App Store payment under Apple’s own privacy policy. Lusa has no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.

Children

Lusa is a stationery tool for people planning an event. It does not offer accounts for children and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

There is no Lusa account to delete. Settings → Clear All Data, after you type PURGE, removes the local draft, your rendered suites, Credits, the welcome-gift flag, and saved transaction identifiers. Demo suites that ship with the app remain. You can also delete Lusa to remove the installation.

Contact

This version does not publish a support or privacy email address. Because Lusa does not receive a copy of your invitations, a privacy request about data on the device is handled by Clear All Data or by deleting the app. For an Apple purchase record, use Apple’s purchase support.
